About Me

Creative, organized, and growing as a designer.

I am a Web and Digital Design student focused on creating clean, useful, and visually engaging digital projects.

My Background

My name is Samantha “Sammy” Krukowski, and I am currently working toward my Bachelor’s degree in Web and Digital Design at the University of Maryland Global Campus. My education has helped me build skills in web design, digital layouts, branding, illustration, user experience, and motion graphics.

I also have professional experience in leadership, customer service, organization, training, and problem solving. These experiences have helped me become dependable, detail-oriented, and comfortable working with both people and deadlines.

Design Focus

I am interested in web and digital design because it allows me to combine creativity with technology. I enjoy creating projects that are organized, easy to use, and visually clear.

My goal is to continue developing a portfolio that shows my ability to create websites, branding materials, digital layouts, and motion-based projects.

What I Want My Portfolio to Show

This portfolio is designed to show my growth as a student and creative professional. Each project was selected because it represents a different part of my education, including web development, graphic design, digital publishing, motion graphics, and visual communication.

I want viewers to see not only the finished projects, but also the skills and creative thinking behind them. My goal is to present my work in a way that feels professional, easy to navigate, and connected to my future career goals.
